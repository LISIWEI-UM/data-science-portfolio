Car sales during recessions

Which vehicle types show the largest gaps in mean sales between recession and other observations?

Open the notebook and Dash app

The gap differs by vehicle type

I grouped 2,112 observations in the course dataset by vehicle type and recession flag. All five categories averaged fewer sales in recession observations. Sports cars showed the largest relative gap, from 3,431 vehicles per observation outside recessions to 815 during recessions, or 76.3% lower. Executive cars followed at 74.7%.

Inside the dashboard

The Dash app offers a recession report and a report for any year from 1980 through 2023. Changing the report updates four charts. The yearly view sums sales within each month and averages sales by vehicle type. In December 2013 the monthly total was 24,360 vehicles.
