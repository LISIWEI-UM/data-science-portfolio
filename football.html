Two ways to count a contribution

Ronaldo scored 935 goals to Messi’s 855 in the source table. Messi recorded 452 assists to Ronaldo’s 303. Adding goals and assists gives Messi 1,307 contributions and Ronaldo 1,238 across their available calendar years from 2002 to 2025.

A peak or a run of high-scoring years?

Messi reached 91 goals in 2012. Ronaldo’s highest year was 2013, with 69. Using 40 goals as a threshold tells a different story about sustained scoring. Ronaldo reached it in 15 years and Messi in 11.

Source and scope

I extended my Practice 07 visualization into a Python workflow that checks years, missing values and a hash of the source observations before aggregating them. The latest run used the fan-maintained table retrieved on 25 November 2026. Four years of records had changed since October. Both snapshots give the same ordering. Snapshot comparison

The source uses a broad definition of an assist. Career lengths, minutes played and competition mix also affect the totals. The next check is to audit annual counts against independent match records and compare a common playing window.
